Privacy

Short version: CodeForge has no account and no analytics, and your code stays with you.

What stays on your device

Projects, settings, chat history and crash reports are stored on your device (Android app) or in your browser (web editor). CodeForge does not upload them to a CodeForge server, and this website does not run analytics.

What leaves your device, and only when you ask

  • AI features. When you send a message, the text and any files or selections you attach go to the AI provider you picked, using your own API key. Their privacy policy applies.
  • Git, extensions and downloads. Cloning, pushing, searching Open VSX or installing packages contacts those services directly. In the web editor, Git can go through a CORS proxy you configure.
  • Links you open in the browser or preview.

API keys

The Android app keeps keys in the Android Keystore and adds them to requests natively. The web editor keeps them in your browser storage, which is less protected.

Crash reports

Reports contain error text, memory figures and recent in-app actions (such as file names). They are saved locally. You choose whether to copy or share one.

Permissions (Android)

Files access (optional) to open folders on your device, network access for the features above, and biometrics if you turn on the app lock.

Contact

Questions about this policy: add a contact address in site-config.js.
